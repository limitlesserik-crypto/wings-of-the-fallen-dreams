A TALE OF LOVE AND SACRIFICE

When the Sky Fell Silent

CHAPTER 05

It was raining heavily that evening. Nat had just finished his shift when his phone rang. It was Mark. "Kuya!" Nat immediately answered. "Mark? What's wrong?" "It's Ella!" Nat stopped walking. "What happened?" "She's not feeling well! Kuya, please come home!" Nat ran. He didn't care about the rain or the exhaustion in his body. All he could think about was Ella. When he arrived, he found his sister being taken to the hospital. "ELLA!" He rushed toward her. His sister opened her eyes slightly. "Kuya..." Nat held her hand. "I'm here. I'm here, Ella." She looked at him weakly. "Kuya, are you tired?" Nat tried to smile. "No, little one. I'm not tired." "You always say that." Nat lowered his head. Ella gently squeezed his hand. "Kuya, don't forget to eat, okay?" He couldn't answer immediately. "Please don't worry about me," he whispered. At the hospital, Nat waited outside the treatment room. Mark sat beside him. For once, his younger brother wasn't playing games or arguing. "Kuya..." Nat looked at him. "Yes?" "Is Ella going to be okay?" Nat stared at the floor. "She has to be." Hours passed. The rain continued outside. Their mother eventually arrived, followed by their father. Nat looked at them. For the first time, he didn't have the strength to argue. He simply wanted his sister to come out of that room. Finally, the doctor approached. Nat immediately stood up. "Doctor? How is my sister?" The doctor paused. He looked at Nat with sympathy. "We're very sorry. Despite our efforts, Ella's condition was too serious. We couldn't save her." The words seemed to stop the entire world. Nat stared at the doctor. "What?" "I'm sorry." "No..." His voice was barely audible. "Please... check again." The doctor lowered his head. Nat couldn't understand what he was hearing. His little sister. The girl who always waited for him after work. The girl who loved biscuits. The girl who dreamed of becoming a teacher. She was gone. Nat slowly entered the room. Ella was lying peacefully. He approached her and held her hand. "Ella?" No response. "Little one?" He waited. He expected her to open her eyes and smile. He expected her to call him Kuya. But the room remained silent. Nat lowered his head. "I'm sorry." His tears fell. "I'm sorry I couldn't buy everything you needed." He tried to speak, but his voice broke. "I'm sorry I couldn't give you the life you deserved." He remembered her dreams. Her laughter. Her little questions. Her promises to buy him a house someday. He remembered every moment he had worked late, every meal he had skipped, every opportunity he had sacrificed. He had done everything he could. But it still wasn't enough. And that was the cruelest part. He had spent so much of his life trying to save someone he loved, only to lose her in the end.
