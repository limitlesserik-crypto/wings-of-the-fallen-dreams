A TALE OF LOVE AND SACRIFICE

The Price of a Family

CHAPTER 03

When Nat arrived home, the house was unusually quiet. He placed his bag beside the door and immediately looked for Ella. "Ella?" His sister appeared from the bedroom. "Kuya!" He smiled and handed her a small plastic bag. "Look what I bought." Ella opened it excitedly. It was a small box of her favorite biscuits. "Kuya! My favorite!" "Don't eat everything at once." "I won't!" Nat laughed. "Where's Mark?" "In his room." Nat walked toward the bedroom and found his middle brother playing games on his phone. "Mark, have you eaten?" "Not yet." "There's some food in the kitchen. Go eat." Mark didn't look up. "Okay." "Also, can you help Ella with her schoolwork tomorrow? I have another shift." Mark finally looked at him. "Why do I always have to do things?" Nat frowned. "Because she's your sister, Mark." "You're always telling me what to do!" Nat remained silent. He didn't want to fight. "Just help her a little. That's all I'm asking." Mark turned away. Nat sighed and went to the kitchen. There, he found his mother preparing to leave. "Mom, where are you going?" "Out." "Did you buy Ella's medicine?" His mother avoided his eyes. "I'll buy it later." Nat looked at the empty table. "Mom, I gave you the money this morning." "Why are you questioning me?" "Because Ella needs her medication!" His voice grew louder. "That money was supposed to be for her!" His mother became defensive. "Don't you dare raise your voice at me!" "Then please tell me where the money went!" His mother grabbed her bag. "I have my own problems, Nat. Stop bothering me." She walked out. Nat stood frozen. He knew where she was going. The gambling place. Again. He went to the living room, where his father was watching television. "Father, did you see what happened?" His father didn't respond. "Please. I'm asking you to do something. Anything. Ella is sick!" His father finally turned toward him. "What do you expect me to do? I don't have a job!" "Then find one!" His father stood up. "Watch your mouth!" Nat looked at him with exhausted eyes. "Do you think I enjoy doing all of this? Do you think I want to spend my youth working instead of studying? I have dreams too, Father!" His voice trembled. "But every time I try to move forward, something pulls me back!" His father said nothing. Nat lowered his head. "I just want Ella to get better." He walked away. Later that night, John called him. "Hey, Nat. How are things?" Nat sat beside the window. "Same as always." "Did you get Ella's medicine?" Nat remained silent. "Nat?" "My mother spent the money again." John sighed. "Man..." "I don't understand. Why is it so difficult for them to care?" John didn't immediately answer. "Listen, you can't keep fixing everything by yourself." "But if I don't, who will?" There was silence. "You're my best friend, Nat. I know you're strong, but even strong people need someone to lean on." Nat looked toward Ella's bedroom. "I don't have time to be weak, John." He ended the call. Then he opened his notebook and began calculating his expenses. Tuition. Transportation. Food. Medicine. Medical appointments. He wrote down every amount until the page was filled. At the bottom, he wrote something else. I hope one day, all of this will be worth it.
