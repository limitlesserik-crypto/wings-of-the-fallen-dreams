BEHIND THE STORY

About the Author

Behind every story is a person with an imagination, a purpose, and something meaningful to share.

Hello! My name is Erik U. Olmeda. I am the writer of the story Wings Of The Fallen Dreams.

This story explores the sacrifices people make for their families, the struggles that often remain unseen, and the emotional weight of responsibilities that one person should never have to carry alone.

Through this work, I hope to encourage readers to show compassion, appreciate the people who care for them, and recognize the importance of family responsibility and understanding.
