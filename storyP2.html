A TALE OF LOVE AND SACRIFICE

A Dream Beneath Exhaustion

CHAPTER 02

At school, Nat sat quietly at the back of the classroom. His eyes were heavy, and his mind was somewhere else. While his classmates discussed their assignments and upcoming activities, he was calculating how much money he needed for Ella's next medical appointment. "Nat!" He looked up. It was John, his best friend. "Are you even listening?" "Sorry. What?" John shook his head. "You're doing it again. You look like you haven't slept in days." "I'm fine." "That's what you always say." Before Nat could respond, their professor entered the room. "Good morning, everyone." The class stood. "Good morning, Professor." After the lesson, the professor announced an upcoming academic scholarship opportunity. "I want everyone to take this seriously. This scholarship could help those who are struggling financially. Some of you have great potential, and I don't want financial problems to become the reason you abandon your education." Nat listened carefully. For a moment, he imagined a different life. A life where he could finish college, find a stable job, provide Ella with proper treatment, and finally give his family a comfortable home. Perhaps, one day, he could stop worrying about tomorrow. "Nat," the professor called. "Yes, Professor?" "I've noticed your recent performance. You are capable of doing better. Is something bothering you?" Nat hesitated. "No, Professor. I'm just a little tired." The professor looked at him with concern. "Remember, being responsible doesn't mean you have to carry everything alone." Nat simply nodded. After class, he met John outside. "Hey, let's eat," John suggested. "I don't have money today." John sighed. "Again?" "I need to save for Ella's medicine." John handed him a sandwich. "Here. Take it." "John, I can't keep accepting your food." "Then consider it a loan. You can pay me back when you're rich." Nat laughed for the first time that day. "You're ridiculous." "And you're stubborn." They continued walking until Nat's phone rang. It was his boss. "Nat, can you come in earlier today? We're short-staffed." Nat checked the time. He still had assignments to finish, but refusing the extra hours meant less money for his family. "I'll be there, sir." At work, Nat immediately changed into his uniform. His boss approached him. "You're working another shift?" "Yes, sir. I need the extra hours." "Don't push yourself too hard. You've been working almost every day." Nat smiled politely. "It's okay, sir." Throughout his shift, he served customers, cleaned tables, arranged supplies, and handled different responsibilities. One customer became irritated after waiting for an order. "How long does it take to prepare one meal? I've been waiting forever!" "I'm sorry, sir. I'll check your order immediately." "You're all so inefficient!" Nat apologized even though the delay wasn't his fault. His boss noticed the situation and approached the customer. Afterward, Nat returned to his duties without complaining. By the time his shift ended, his legs were aching. But he still had to go home. He still had assignments. And Ella still needed her medicine. For Nat, resting was a luxury he could rarely afford.
